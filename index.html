Featured · Final-year project

Swing-Powered Electrical Generator

A kinetic energy harvester that converts the back-and-forth motion of a swing into electrical energy for USB charging.

  • Modelled the swing as a nonlinear pendulum in MATLAB/Simulink to understand its motion and size the drivetrain.
  • A bicycle freewheel turns the two-way swing motion into one-way rotation, driving a 320 RPM PMDC generator through a chain drive.
  • Redesigned during the build: welded a 50T sprocket onto the freewheel to raise the gear ratio, and machined the shaft to fit.
  • Output conditioned on stripboard and boosted through an MT3608 module to a USB output.
